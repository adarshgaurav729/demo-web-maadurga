## 5. Website Architecture & Pages
- **Technology:** Pure HTML5, CSS3, and modern Vanilla JavaScript (no frameworks, no build tools, zero npm dependencies, instant static hosting).
- **Pages & Components:**
  - `index.html`: High-impact Homepage with emergency/offer notification bar, Hero banner with top seasonal promotions, category cards, featured smartphones & home appliances, dedicated Triveni Almirah showcase, trust badges, customer testimonials, interactive store locator & Google Map, and comprehensive footer.
  - `shop.html`: Complete product catalog with real-time category filtering (All, Smartphones, Smart TVs, Triveni Almirahs, Home Appliances, Accessories), sorting (price, popularity), live search bar, and quick-enquiry cards.
  - `about.html`: Heritage of Maa Durga Enterprises, showroom overview, local community commitment, and authenticity promise.
  - `contact.html`: Store visit guide, direct one-click WhatsApp/Call links, timing details, interactive Google Map, and inquiry form.
